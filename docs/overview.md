# Overview

Nook is a team workspace for conversations that lead to clear decisions and next steps. It brings private channels, fixed-participant direct conversations, threads, reactions, files, decisions, commitments and attention into one place.

## Who it is for

Nook is for teams that want a shared place to talk in channels, keep a conversation with its replies, record a decision and assign a commitment. Each person sees the conversations they currently belong to.

Start with [installing Nook](install.md), then learn about [your team](your-team.md) and [conversations](conversations.md).

## What Nook does

Nook keeps conversation history, decisions and commitments in the workspace. Decisions retain their versions. Only the assigned person can accept a commitment and report it complete; Nook records that report without independently verifying the work. [Attention](attention.md) helps each person follow the conversations and requests they choose.

## What Nook does not provide

Nook has no AI feature. Sign-in, invitation and opted-in notification email uses the mail provider configured by the workspace owner. Optional Web Push sends encrypted alerts directly through your browser’s push service. Nook can be installed as a progressive web app, but its service worker caches no requests or private data, so it requires a network connection. Nook does not claim that a local installation is ready for hosted production use.

## What has not been proven

Hosted operation on a real Cloudflare account and live mail delivery, capacity beyond the recorded local measurement, native mobile push and real-device Web Push receipt (email is proven against the local development outbox; Web Push has a local encrypted network-edge proof), integrations, and a full accessibility audit have not been proven. Local synthetic verification is not a production, parity or adoption claim.
