# Channel policy and history

Open a channel’s **Details** to edit its settings. Its current manager can save a description of up to 2,000 characters, distinct from the topic. The description also appears in Browse public channels and travels with readable exports and operational backups.

## Workspace policy

Owners see a Workspace policy section in Channel details. Admins can read it; members and guests do not see it. Only owners can save it while delegation is unavailable.

Choose separately who may create public channels, create private channels and archive channels: owners, owners and admins, or all full members. Guests are excluded. Before an owner first saves a policy, full members can create channels and the existing channel management rules govern archive. Saving changes later operations, leaving existing channels and memberships intact. A stale policy version must be reloaded before saving. Each change has one administrator audit entry. Backups preserve the exact policy.

## Make a private channel public

A native private channel’s current manager can preview conversion in Details. The preview names the exact visible message and attached-file counts, notes, earliest message date, and all current full members who can gain access by joining. All history becomes visible workspace-wide and cannot be re-hidden. Read the preview and explicitly confirm that disclosure to proceed. New history or a membership change requires a fresh preview.

Conversion keeps current members, posts a visible system notice and records an administrator audit entry. The manager must be permitted to post the notice. Erased content stays erased and is excluded from the visible counts. Imported channels, channels moved from group DMs and channels with retained-erasure bodies cannot currently be converted; their custody stays private. Legal holds remain unbuilt under NOOK-039. Public-to-private conversion is unavailable.

Delegated policy permissions, custom roles and groups, guest expiry, invitation/domain policy and links, message edit/delete windows, custom emoji, group defaults and organization notification defaults remain incomplete. Existing posting and broadcast settings retain their own controls.
