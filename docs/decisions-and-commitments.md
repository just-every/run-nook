# Decisions and commitments

Record a decision or propose a commitment from a conversation. Both keep a version history.

Hover over a message (or tap it on a phone) and choose **Record decision** or **Ask someone**, which quotes the message it starts from. The **Decisions** button in the conversation header opens the panel that lists everything for that conversation: what is waiting on you, other open requests, decisions, and a collapsed list of closed items. The button shows how many are open and a dot when something is waiting on you. A decision or request made from a message also shows as a short line under that message; choose it to jump to its card.

## Decisions

A decision records what the team chose. Its text and versions stay with the conversation where it was created.

## Commitments

A commitment has one assignee. Only that assignee can accept the proposal, and **Accept** and **Report done** appear only for them. After accepting it, the assignee can report it complete. Hover over a card for **Discuss** and, for its author, the **More** menu with **Revise** and **Cancel request**. Nook records the completion report; it does not verify that the work was done.

Changing an unfinished commitment creates a new version and sends a fresh attention item to the person who needs to act. See [Attention](attention.md) for following and clearing those items.
